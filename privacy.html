Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and how to ask us to remove it.

Last updated:

Bridget's Boutique ("we," "us," "our") is a women's clothing boutique in Enumclaw, Washington. This page explains what information this website collects when you use it, what we do with it, and how you can reach us about it. It covers only bridgetsboutiqueenumclaw.com — not our Instagram, Facebook, or in-store transactions, which follow those platforms' or that process's own rules.

What we collect

The only information this site collects is what you choose to give us by filling out the contact / order form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • The item you're asking about, if any
  • Your message

We don't ask for payment details, a password, or a shipping address through this site — orders and payment are handled in person or by phone. Browsing the site, viewing products, and reading our story collect nothing at all: there is no account to create and no tracking script running in the background.

How we use it

Only to answer you. Your message goes to a private dashboard that only Bridget's Boutique staff can open, so we can follow up about your inquiry or order. We don't add you to a mailing list, sell your information, or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in Google Firebase, a cloud database. Access is restricted to authorized staff — no one else can read, edit, or export it, and it can never be changed from a visitor's browser, only read by our team.

Other services this site uses

A few pages load content from other companies, each governed by their own privacy policy rather than ours:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this page are served from Google's servers.
  • Google Maps — our About page embeds a map of the store.
  • Instagram — our home page embeds a post from our Instagram account.

Loading these pages may send your browser's IP address and similar technical information to Google or Meta as part of how those embeds work, the same as visiting google.com or instagram.com directly. We don't control what those companies do with that.

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies or use any analytics or advertising tracking. The Google Maps and Instagram embeds mentioned above may set their own cookies as part of loading; that's between your browser and them, not something we receive.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete any information you've sent us — call (360) 625-8032 or use the contact form. We'll take care of it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top of it.

Questions

Call us at (360) 625-8032, message us on Instagram or Facebook, or stop by 1617 Cole St, Enumclaw, WA 98022.
